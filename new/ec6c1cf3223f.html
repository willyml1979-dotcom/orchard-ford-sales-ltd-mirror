Experience the 2027 Ford Super Duty F-350 SRW Platinum: a rugged yet refined 4x4 crew cab pickup built for power, comfort, and advanced off-road capability.

This Star White Metallic Tri-Coat truck is powered by a robust 6.7L HO Power Stroke V8 Turbo Diesel engine paired with a TorqShift 10-speed automatic transmission featuring selectable drive modes including tow/haul and off-road. The Tremor Off-Road Package enhances its capability with unique front springs for increased ride height, front limited slip differential, performance shocks, rock crawl mode, trail control, and skid plates. Ebony black machined 18-inch aluminum wheels with all-terrain tires and textured matte off-road running boards complete the tough exterior.

Inside, the Black Onyx and Platinum Blue cabin boasts unique Platinum leather 40/console/40 seats with multi-contour heating, ventilation, and massage functions. Both driver and passenger enjoy 8-way power-adjustable seats with lumbar support and memory settings. The twin-panel power moonroof adds natural light, while the Pro Power Onboard system delivers 2kW of power for tools and devices. Safety is comprehensive with blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, and adaptive cruise control. Additional features include a 6-switch upfitter panel, SecuriCode keyless entry keypad, panoramic roof, and all-weather floor mats for durability. This F-350 Platinum is engineered to meet the demands of heavy-duty work and off-road adventures without sacrificing luxury.
