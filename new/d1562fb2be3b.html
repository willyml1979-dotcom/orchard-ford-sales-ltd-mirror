Power, precision, and advanced towing technology define the 2027 Ford Super Duty F-350 DRW LARIAT in Star White Metallic Tri-Coat, designed for demanding work and rugged terrain.

This 4x4 Crew Cab Pickup with an 8-foot long bed is powered by a robust 6.7L HO Power Stroke V8 Turbo Diesel engine paired with a TorqShift 10-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift and multiple drive modes including tow/haul and off-road. Dual rear wheels and a locking limited-slip differential enhance stability and traction, while the FX4 Off-Road Package adds rock crawl drive mode, hill descent control, premium off-road shocks, and skid plates for serious off-road capability.

Towing is made effortless with the Pro Trailer Hitch Assist, Pro Trailer Backup Assist, and the 5th Wheel/Gooseneck Hitch Prep Package, complete with integrated connectors and bed attachment points. The LARIAT Premium Package includes Ford Co-Pilot 360 Assist 2.0 with adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping system, front and rear parking sensors, automatic emergency braking with pedestrian detection, and driver monitoring for enhanced safety.

Inside, six passengers ride comfortably on Black Onyx ActiveX-trimmed seats with driver lumbar adjustment and pass-through rear bench seating. Convenience is boosted by a twin-panel power moonroof, power-sliding rear window with defrost, and six overhead upfitter switches for custom equipment control. Rugged all-terrain tires, black platform running boards, and heavy-duty suspension complete this capable workhorse.
