Experience the power and versatility of the 2027 Ford F-150 LARIAT Super Crew, featuring advanced towing capabilities and innovative bed utility enhancements.

This new Ford F-150 LARIAT is equipped with a robust 3.5L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission, offering selectable drive modes including tow/haul, sport, and deep snow/sand for adaptable performance in any condition. The 4WD system with locking limited-slip differential ensures confident handling on rugged terrain. The truck’s 5.5-foot short bed comes with the Bed Utility Package, which includes LED box lighting, bed storage boxes, four tie-down plates, and an integrated tailgate step with a work surface—perfect for job sites or weekend projects. Extended 6-inch running boards add convenience for easy access.

Inside, premium synthetic bucket seats provide heating, cooling, and power adjustments with memory settings for driver and passenger comfort. The panoramic roof and generic sun/moonroof enhance the spacious black interior. Safety is comprehensive, featuring blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, adaptive cruise control, front and rear collision mitigation, and a driver monitoring system. Additional tech includes an aerial view display system, remote engine start, and a premium sound system. With integrated trailer brake controller and tow hooks, this F-150 is ready for serious hauling and everyday driving alike.
