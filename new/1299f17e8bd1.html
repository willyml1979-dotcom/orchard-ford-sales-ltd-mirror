Experience the power and versatility of the 2027 Ford Super Duty F-350 SRW XLT in Carbonized Grey Metallic, designed for demanding work and rugged terrain.

This crew cab pickup features a robust 6.7L HO Power Stroke V8 Turbo Diesel engine paired with a TorqShift 10-speed automatic transmission, offering selectable drive modes including tow/haul and off-road for precise control. The 4x4 drivetrain with locking limited-slip differential and FX4 Off-Road Package ensures confidence on challenging surfaces, enhanced by hill descent control, premium off-road shock absorbers, and skid plates.

Inside, the spacious cabin seats six and includes driver adjustable lumbar support and a pass-through rear bench seat. The XLT Premium Package adds heated front seats, an electrochromic self-dimming rearview mirror, and PowerScope trailer tow mirrors with memory and LED utility lighting. Stay connected with SYNC 4 and a 12-inch centre display, complemented by a premium B&O sound system with HD radio and subwoofer.

Safety is comprehensive with automatic headlights, high beams, cross-traffic alert, rear collision mitigation, and a 360-degree camera system with rear parking sensors and reverse brake assist. Additional features include Pro Power Onboard 2kW dual alternators, six upfitter switches, integrated tailgate step, and a SecuriCode keyless entry keypad for convenience and security.
