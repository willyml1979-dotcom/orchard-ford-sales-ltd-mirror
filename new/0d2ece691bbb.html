Experience the 2027 Ford Explorer ST in Avalanche Grey, a powerful 4x4 SUV designed for drivers who demand performance, comfort, and advanced safety.

Powered by a robust 3.0L EcoBoost V6 engine with auto start-stop technology, paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift capability and paddle shifters, this Explorer ST delivers responsive and efficient driving. The ST Sinister Package enhances its sporty appeal with 21" gloss black aluminum wheels, 275/45R21 all-season performance tires, and distinctive LED daytime running lights with amber and red ST badging.

Inside, the spacious black interior comfortably seats six with premium synthetic bucket seats, including heated and cooled front seats, heated rear seats, and adjustable lumbar support for both driver and passenger. Enjoy added luxury with seat memory and seat-massage functions, complemented by a leather steering wheel with heating for cold Canadian mornings.

Safety is paramount with features like Blind Spot Monitor, Front Collision Mitigation, Lane Keeping Assist, Rear Collision Mitigation, and Cross-Traffic Alert. Additional convenience comes from the panoramic sun/moonroof, hands-free power liftgate, remote engine start, and an aerial view display system for confident manoeuvring. Equipped with a tow hitch and aluminum wheels, this Explorer ST is ready for adventure and everyday versatility.
