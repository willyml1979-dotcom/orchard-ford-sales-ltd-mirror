Experience commanding performance and refined luxury with the 2027 Ford Expedition Platinum Max in Glacier Grey Metallic Tri-Coat, designed for drivers who demand capability and comfort in every journey.

Powered by a turbocharged V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift, this 4x4 SUV delivers smooth, responsive handling on any terrain. The Stealth Appearance Package adds a bold, sophisticated edge with black Platinum badging, ebony trim headlamps, carbon black front bumper, and 22-inch Magnetite painted aluminum wheels wrapped in all-season tires. Running boards and a rear spoiler enhance both style and functionality.

Inside, the spacious black leather interior accommodates seven with heated and cooled front bucket seats, heated rear seats, and power-adjustable driver and passenger seats with lumbar support and memory settings. The Driver’s Package elevates convenience with a power panoramic Vista roof, power tilt/telescoping steering column with memory, wireless charging pad, and power-folding sideview mirrors with auto-dimming and memory functions.

Safety is comprehensive, featuring blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, and a suite of airbags. Advanced technology includes an aerial view display system, adaptive cruise control, B&O Play Unleashed premium sound with 22 speakers, and a hands-free power liftgate. Tow hitch and tire pressure monitoring complete this commanding SUV built for confident driving.
