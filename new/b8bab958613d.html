Experience commanding performance and refined comfort with the 2027 Ford Expedition Tremor, designed for drivers who demand capability and luxury in every journey.

This new Ford Expedition Tremor features a robust turbocharged V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission with SelectShift capability, delivering smooth power and responsive control on all terrains. Its advanced 4x4 drivetrain includes a locking limited-slip differential, perfect for tackling challenging road conditions. The exterior shines in Star White Metallic Tri-Coat, complemented by aluminum wheels and all-terrain tires front and rear, along with running boards for easy access. A panoramic sun/moonroof enhances the spacious cabin, while the illuminated rear spoiler adds a sporty touch.

Inside, enjoy premium leather-trimmed heated and ventilated front captain’s chairs in Black Onyx/Electric Spice, with power-adjustable driver and passenger seats featuring memory and lumbar support. The driver’s package includes a power tilt/telescoping steering column with memory, heated power-folding sideview mirrors with ground illumination, and a wireless charging pad. The B&O Play Unleashed sound system with 22 speakers offers a customizable audio experience tailored to your mood.

Safety is comprehensive with features such as blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, adaptive cruise control, and an aerial view display system. The Expedition Tremor is ready to elevate your driving experience with power, technology, and comfort.
