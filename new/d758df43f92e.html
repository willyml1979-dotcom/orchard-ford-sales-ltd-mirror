Experience the 2027 Ford F-150 LARIAT in Birch Sand, a rugged yet refined Super Crew truck designed for versatility and advanced capability on any terrain.

Powered by a 3.5L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift and multiple drive modes, this 4WD pickup adapts seamlessly from sport to tow/haul, deep snow, or off-road conditions. The FX4 Off-Road Package enhances durability with rock crawl mode, off-road tuned front shocks, skid plates protecting vital components, monotube rear shocks, hill descent control, and an electronic locking rear differential with a 3.31 axle ratio. The Bed Utility Package adds LED box lighting, storage boxes, tie-down plates, an integrated tailgate step with work surface, and 6-inch extended running boards for easy access and cargo management. Tow with confidence using the integrated trailer brake controller from the Tow/Haul Package.

Inside, premium synthetic seats offer heating, cooling, and power adjustments with memory and lumbar support for both driver and passenger, ensuring comfort on long hauls. The panoramic roof and generic sun/moonroof flood the black interior with natural light. Safety is comprehensive, including blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front collision mitigation, and a driver monitoring system. This F-150 is built to perform, protect, and provide a commanding presence wherever your journey takes you.
