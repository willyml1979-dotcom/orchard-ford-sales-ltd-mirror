Experience the 2027 Ford F-150 XLT in Carbonized Grey Metallic with advanced towing, safety, and comfort features designed for demanding Canadian roads and rugged adventures.

This Super Crew 4x4 pickup combines a powerful 5.0L V8 engine with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift and multiple drive modes, including tow/haul and deep snow/sand, ensuring optimal performance in any terrain. The 5.5-foot short bed comes equipped with a remote tailgate release and 400W Pro Power Onboard in both cab and bed, perfect for worksite power needs. Safety is paramount with Ford Co-Pilot360 Assist 2.0, which includes adaptive cruise control with stop & go, lane centring, predictive speed assist, automatic emergency braking oncoming, blind spot monitoring, cross-traffic alert, and a 360-degree camera system complemented by front parking sensors.

The XLT Black Appearance Package adds a bold look with a black grille, black badging, 18-inch gloss black wheels, black running boards, and dark interior appliques. Comfort features include heated front seats, dual-zone electronic temperature control, power sliding privacy glass rear window with defroster, and power-folding heated side mirrors with LED spotlights. Additional conveniences include remote start, pro trailer backup assist, and a locking limited-slip differential for enhanced traction and control. This F-150 is built to handle both work and play with confidence and style.
