Experience the 2027 Ford Transit Cargo Van in Oxford White, designed for reliability and efficiency with advanced safety and powerful performance features.

Powered by a robust 3.5L EcoBoost V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift and an auxiliary transmission oil cooler, this full-size cargo van delivers confident all-wheel drive capability with a 3.73 limited-slip axle ratio. The midship extended range fuel tank holds 117 litres, complemented by a capless fuel fill for convenience on long hauls. Inside, the dark Palazzo grey cloth bucket seats offer 2-way manual adjustments with lumbar support and armrests, ensuring comfort during extended drives. The spacious cargo area is illuminated by full rear compartment LED lighting positioned at the C-pillar, D-pillar, mid-ship, and controlled by a rear compartment switch, enhancing visibility when loading or unloading.

Safety is paramount with features including automatic headlights, automatic high beams, front collision mitigation, lane departure warning, lane keeping assist, and a comprehensive airbag system. Additional aids like brake assist, traction and stability control, and a locking limited-slip differential provide enhanced control. Practical exterior elements include a third passenger door and all-season tires front and rear. Convenience is further supported by keyless entry and start, back-up camera, rear parking aid, Bluetooth connectivity, WiFi hotspot, and steering wheel audio controls, making this van a versatile partner for any business.
